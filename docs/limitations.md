# Sondera — Explicit Limitations & Honesty Disclosures

In accordance with Sondera's core philosophy—**explainability before verdicts**—the following explicit limitations must be understood:

## 1. No Guarantee of Safety
- A scan that yields zero findings does **NOT** certify that a message, URL, or sender is safe or legitimate.
- Attackers continuously formulate polite, low-pressure, or novel phrasing that bypasses static heuristic rules.
- Absence of detected warning patterns is never proof of authenticity.

## 2. HTTPS Does Not Equal Honesty
- Modern phishing and scam websites routinely acquire valid, free SSL/TLS certificates (e.g. via Let's Encrypt).
- HTTPS ensures encryption in transit; it does not attest to the legal or ethical intentions of the domain operator.

## 3. Absence of Cryptographic Telecom Verification
- The local text engine cannot authenticate the cryptographic origins of SMS shortcodes or email headers.
- Telecom caller ID spoofing and display name manipulation cannot be detected through text appearance alone.

## 4. Trust Debt is Explanatory, Not Probabilistic
- Trust Debt is an explanatory metric documenting missing evidence and circulation ratios.
- It is not a calibrated mathematical probability of scam likelihood.

## 5. Educational Content is Not Live Threat Intelligence
- Scenarios in the **Judgment Gym** and sample intake buttons are curated educational exercises, not live malicious infrastructure.
